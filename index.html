BE FILLED WITH THE SPIRIT 🫵 "For David himself said by the Holy Ghost, The Lord said to my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand, till I make thine enemies thy footstool. Mark 12:36" God is good, and His goodness will never cease. He sent the Holy Spirit to work with the Old Testament prophets to give direction and training to the Old Testament saints and the nation of Israel at large. In our new dispensation, He has sent the Holy Spirit to take permanent residence on Earth till the second coming of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ (Acts 2:2-4). Dear beloved, desire to be filled with the Holy Spirit, and ask Him to fill you; if you are born again, you qualify to be filled. Being filled with the Holy Spirit comes with the language of the Spirit, which is tongues and prophesy. The infilling of the Holy Spirit brings empowerment, which will cause you to receive revelations from God, do righteousness, intercede for other in prayer, preach the gospel, and become immovable for Christ. Hallelujah! Walk in the Spirit!
